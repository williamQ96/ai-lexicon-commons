WORKING DEFINITION

Foundation model

A model trained on broad data and intended to support adaptation to a range of downstream tasks.

Proposed; pending team confirmation.

Scope

A model's training breadth and reuse across tasks, following the terminology introduced by Bommasani and colleagues.

Includes

  • Adaptation by fine-tuning or other task-specific use of broadly trained models.

Excludes

  • A synonym for every pretrained model, every large model, or every language model.
  • A guarantee of generality, reliability, or fitness for a particular task.

Ambiguities and boundaries

  • Breadth and adaptability are contextual; this draft sets no universal size or task-count threshold.
  • The introducing report is a research report/preprint. Its terminology is attributed, rather than presented as disciplinary consensus.

Operational criteria

Document the training scope available from public sources and the downstream tasks or adaptation settings that motivate the label. Mark unknown training details as unknown.

This project's chosen working meaning

Project choice: keep broad training and downstream adaptability as the core meaning; omit a fixed parameter threshold. Pending team confirmation.
