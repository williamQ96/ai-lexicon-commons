WORKING DEFINITION

AI engineering

The engineering practice of designing, integrating, evaluating, deploying, and maintaining AI systems so they serve stated needs in their operating context.

Proposed; pending team confirmation.

Scope

System delivery and operation across the lifecycle, including software, data, models, infrastructure, and people.

Includes

  • Work on reliability, scalability, security, human interaction, and operational evaluation.

Excludes

  • A synonym limited to training a new model or writing prompts.
  • A claim that one organization's framework is a universal standard.

Ambiguities and boundaries

  • Some communities use AI engineering for application development around existing models; CMU SEI describes a broader lifecycle discipline.
  • State whether a discussion concerns model development, application integration, or the complete operational system.

Operational criteria

Identify the system's intended users and outcomes, evaluation criteria, deployment context, monitoring, and maintenance responsibilities. Model performance alone is not the whole acceptance criterion.

This project's chosen working meaning

Project choice: retain the broader lifecycle meaning informed by CMU SEI. Narrower application-development usage remains relevant when explicitly qualified; team confirmation is pending.
