WORKING DEFINITION

Operational definition

A specification of how a concept is observed, measured, or classified in a stated setting, including the rule for interpreting observations.

Proposed; pending team confirmation.

Scope

Procedures intended to support repeatable measurement or classification.

Includes

  • Observation units, inputs, procedure, decision rules, and treatment of missing or ambiguous cases.

Excludes

  • A concept description without a procedure.
  • A claim that specifying a procedure alone establishes scientific validity.

Ambiguities and boundaries

  • Different procedures can operationalize the same concept differently. State the setting and limits instead of treating the procedure as universal.

Operational criteria

Identify units and inputs, give executable steps, state thresholds and units where used, and explain missing and ambiguous cases. Add synthetic boundary examples when useful; mark unsettled rules pending.
