WORKING DEFINITION

Provenance

Information about an entity's origin and production history, including relevant activities and responsible agents.

Proposed; pending team confirmation.

Scope

Tracing the derivation of a public definition, document, or research object, informed by W3C PROV-O.

Includes

  • Public source identifiers and versions, derivation steps, and attribution.

Excludes

  • An unsupported origin claim or a guarantee of accuracy.
  • Disclosure of restricted source material.

Ambiguities and boundaries

  • A source citation is part of provenance, but may omit transformations or decisions between the source and the resulting object.
  • Recording provenance does not by itself verify a claim or imply conformance to the full PROV standard.

Operational criteria

Record a public source or identify an original proposal, the source version/date, a locator where available, and the adaptation. Mark provenance gaps unknown.

This project's chosen working meaning

Project choice: use a lightweight origin-and-derivation meaning informed by the 2013 W3C Recommendation; no full-standard conformance is claimed. Pending team confirmation.
