WORKING DEFINITION

Working definition

An explicit, revisable statement of the meaning chosen for a term within a stated context.

Proposed; pending team confirmation.

Scope

The meanings used in this glossary and in discussions that explicitly reference an entry.

Includes

  • The intended referent, context, and boundaries needed to interpret the term.

Excludes

  • A claim of universal agreement, disciplinary consensus, or lab ratification.

Ambiguities and boundaries

  • Chosen for use in this draft and adopted by the team are different statuses. A draft can state a concrete meaning while confirmation remains pending.

Operational criteria

State the intended meaning and scope; identify inclusions and exclusions. Add operational criteria where measurement or classification is intended, or explain why they do not apply.
