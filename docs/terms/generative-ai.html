WORKING DEFINITION

Generative AI

AI used to produce content such as text, images, audio, or code, commonly in response to prompts or other conditioning inputs.

Proposed; pending team confirmation.

Scope

The content-generation capability or use of an AI system, informed by the OECD policy paper.

Includes

  • Systems generating one or several content modalities.

Excludes

  • A synonym for all AI, all machine learning, or every predictive system.
  • A claim that generated content is original, correct, or grounded in evidence.

Ambiguities and boundaries

  • Generative AI describes a capability or use, while foundation model describes training and adaptability; the labels answer different questions.
  • A system may combine generation with retrieval, classification, and other functions.

Operational criteria

Describe the generated output, conditioning inputs, and how generation is used in the system. Evaluate quality or correctness separately from the label.

This project's chosen working meaning

Project choice: classify the relevant capability or use as generative, rather than treating the label as a complete description of an application. Pending team confirmation.
