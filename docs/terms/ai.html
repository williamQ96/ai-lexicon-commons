WORKING DEFINITION

AI

Artificial intelligence

AI refers here to the research and engineering of machine-based systems that infer from inputs how to produce outputs toward explicit or implicit objectives.

Proposed; pending team confirmation.

Scope

For an implementation, use AI system. The system sense follows the OECD framing: outputs may be predictions, content, recommendations, or decisions, and autonomy and adaptation vary.

Includes

  • Machine-learning and knowledge-based approaches within the stated system context.

Excludes

  • An assumption that AI means only generative AI or only language models.
  • Claims about consciousness, human equivalence, or a legal classification.

Ambiguities and boundaries

  • AI can name a field, a capability, a model, or an entire system. Specify which sense is intended.
  • The OECD definition serves its recommendation's purpose. This glossary uses it as a conceptual reference; it does not resolve every boundary with ordinary software.

Operational criteria

When describing an AI system, identify its inputs, objectives, output types, system boundary, and degree of human involvement. Record borderline classifications and their rationale.

This project's chosen working meaning

Project choice: use AI as the umbrella term; use AI system for a concrete implementation. This is a paraphrase and scoped interpretation of the OECD definition, pending team confirmation.
